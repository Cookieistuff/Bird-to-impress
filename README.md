# 🐦 Bird to Impress

A 3D bird dress-up game in the style of **Dress to Impress**. You can play it in your browser (it works on iPad too).

## ▶️ Play
**https://cookieistuff.github.io/bird-to-impress/**
(To turn this link on, open the repo's Settings → Pages, then set Branch to `main` and the folder to `/root`.)

## ✨ Features
- **20 birds** you can play as: Cookie, Snowflake, Mini Snowflake, Lightning, Salman, Lemon, Jafar, Pikachu, Silver, Abbas, plus a Budgie, Macaw, Cockatoo, Toucan, Penguin, Owl, Flamingo, Peacock, Pigeon, Canary and Robin
- **🏆 Compete mode**: get a theme and 90 seconds to dress up, then walk the runway, vote on the other birds with stars, and finish on the podium
- **Tournaments** of 1, 3, 5 or 10 rounds that add up points from every round
- **32 themes**, for example Pirate Party, Royal Ball, Under the Sea, Robot Future, Dino Days and Circus Clowns
- **Over 120 clothing items** to mix and match, and most of them can be recolored
- **14 places**: Studio, Castle, Space, Pirate Ship, Beach, Disco, Spooky Night, Snowy Land, Under the Sea, Farm, Jungle, Circus, Hero City
- 🎭 Ready-made character looks, 🎲 random outfits, 💃 a dance mode with music, 📸 photo mode

## 🎮 Controls
Drag to spin your bird · pinch to zoom · tap to make it chirp

## 🛠️ Made with
A single `index.html` file that uses [three.js](https://threejs.org/), so there's nothing to build or install.
